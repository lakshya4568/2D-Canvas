PURPOSE
-------
A Pre-Stressed Concrete (PSC) Slab Bridge uses pre-tensioned or post-tensioned
concrete solid or U-shaped slabs as superstructure over piers/abutments.
Common standard spans: 3.05m, 3.66m, 4.57m, 6.10m, 9.15m, 12.2m, 18.3m.

SECTION 2: SPAN ARRANGEMENT GEOMETRY
---------------------------------------

--- FORMULA PSC-GEO-001 ---
Name:         Number of Spans and Piers
Formula:      nS = spans.length
              nP = nS - 1
Output:       nS = number of spans
              nP = number of intermediate piers

--- FORMULA PSC-GEO-002 ---
Name:         Centre-to-Centre Span Length for Position Calculation
Formula:      IF (first AND last span, i.e. single span):
                cToc = clearSpan
              IF first span (not last):
                cToc = clearSpan + 0.5325
              IF last span (not first):
                cToc = clearSpan + 0.5325
              IF middle span:
                cToc = clearSpan + capWidth
Output:       cToc [m]  Effective c/c bearing length for positioning
Explanation:
  0.5325m = abutment face offset (0.1725m) + half pier cap contribution.
  For middle spans, a full capWidth offset is added on each side.
Source:       psc-slab-rule-engine.ts lines 130-143

--- FORMULA PSC-GEO-003 ---
Name:         Total Bridge Length
Formula:      totalLength = sum of all cToc values
            = positions[nS]    (last position in the accumulated array)
Output:       totalLength [m]  Total bridge longitudinal length

--- FORMULA PSC-GEO-004 ---
Name:         RDSO Pier Cap Width
Formula:      rdso_cap_w = deck_w + 2 * inspection_path_w
            = deck_w + 2 * 0.608
Output:       rdso_cap_w [m]  Minimum pier cap width per RDSO standard
Note:         The 0.608m inspection walkway is required on each side.


SECTION 3: SLAB DIMENSIONS (RDSO STANDARD LOOKUP)
---------------------------------------------------

--- FORMULA PSC-SLAB-001 ---
Name:         RDSO Slab Depth at Centre (Post-tensioned, by span)
Table:        clearSpan  -> slabD_center (m)
              3.05m      -> 0.365m (or 0.350m pretensioned)
              3.66m      -> 0.425m (or 0.410m pretensioned)
              4.57m      -> 0.425m (post-tensioned) / 0.490m (pretensioned) / 0.300m (U-slab)
              6.10m      -> 0.575m (post-tensioned) / 0.560m (pretensioned) / 0.300m (U-slab)
              9.15m      -> 0.830m (post-tensioned) / 0.780m (pretensioned) / 0.350m (U-slab)
              12.2m      -> 0.850m / 1.100m (RDSO/B-10271R) / 0.350m (U-slab)
              18.3m      -> 1.250m
Source:       psc-slab-rule-engine.ts getSlabDimensions() lines 26-79

--- FORMULA PSC-SLAB-002 ---
Name:         RDSO Slab Depth at Support
Table:        clearSpan  -> slabD_support (m)
              3.05m      -> 0.365m (post-tensioned) / 0.280m (pretensioned)
              3.66m      -> 0.425m (post-tensioned) / 0.325m (pretensioned)
              4.57m      -> 0.425m (post-tensioned) / 0.400m (pretensioned) / 0.300m (U-slab)
              6.10m      -> 0.460m (post-tensioned, default) / 0.460m (pretensioned) / 0.300m (U-slab)
              9.15m      -> 0.600m (post-tensioned) / 0.560m (pretensioned) / 0.350m (U-slab)
              12.2m      -> 0.850m / 0.350m (U-slab)
              18.3m      -> 1.250m

--- FORMULA PSC-SLAB-003 ---
Name:         PSC Slab Camber
Formula:      camber = slabD_center - slabD_support
Output:       camber [m]  Mid-span upward deflection (camber) of the pre-stressed slab
Explanation:
  PSC slabs are pre-stressed (stressed before use), giving them an upward bow
  (camber) at mid-span. The camber equals the difference in slab depth
  between centre and support sections.
Source:       psc-slab-rule-engine.ts lines 72-77

--- FORMULA PSC-SLAB-004 ---
Name:         PSC Overall Slab Length (by type and span)
Table (post-tensioned):
              3.05m -> 3.940m   |   3.66m -> 4.555m   |   4.57m -> 5.545m
              6.10m -> 7.145m   |   9.15m -> 10.280m  |   12.2m -> 13.380m
Table (pretensioned):
              3.05m -> 3.940m   |   3.66m -> 4.555m   |   4.57m -> 5.545m
              6.10m -> 7.145m   |   9.15m -> 10.280m
Table (U-slab):
              4.57m -> 5.545m   |   6.10m -> 7.145m   |   9.15m -> 10.280m
              12.2m -> 13.375m
General:      overallLength = clearSpan + 2 * overhang
              overhang = stemThick - dirtWallWidth - gap
              gap = 0.020m (construction tolerance)
Source:       psc-slab-rule-engine.ts getPSCSlabOverallLength() lines 343-370

--- FORMULA PSC-SLAB-005 ---
Name:         Minimum Slab Thickness (IRS T-39 Rule)
Formula:      minSlabThk = maxSpan / 25
Output:       minSlabThk [m]  RDSO minimum required slab depth
Standard:     IRS T-39 Clause — slab thickness >= L/25
Validation:   slab.thickness >= maxSpan / 25

--- FORMULA PSC-SLAB-006 ---
Name:         Maximum Slab Thickness (RDSO Guideline)
Formula:      prefT = maxSpan / 12
Output:       prefT [m]  Upper recommended limit on slab depth
Note:         If slab thickness > L/12, the section may be over-designed.

--- FORMULA PSC-SLAB-007 ---
Name:         Wearing Coat Thickness (by type and span)
Formula:      wcT = 0.060m  (default for most spans)
              wcT = 0.080m  (for U-slab at 4.57m, 6.1m, 9.15m, 12.2m)
              wcT = user_input (if explicitly provided)
Output:       wcT [m]  Wearing coat / protective concrete layer thickness


SECTION 4: VERTICAL LEVEL COMPUTATION (PSC SLAB)
--------------------------------------------------

--- FORMULA PSC-LVL-001 ---
Name:         Rail Level to Top of Slab (Railway Deck)
Formula:      z_slab_t = z_formation - wcT - camber
Output:       z_slab_t [m]  Top of slab level
Explanation:
  For railway bridges, the slab top sits below the formation level by
  the wearing coat thickness and the pre-stress camber.
  z_formation = rail_level (z_top for railway decks)

--- FORMULA PSC-LVL-002 ---
Name:         Bottom of Slab (Railway Deck)
Formula:      z_slab_b = z_slab_t - slabD_support
Output:       z_slab_b [m]  Underside of PSC slab at support

--- FORMULA PSC-LVL-003 ---
Name:         Top of Bearing
Formula:      z_bearing_t = z_slab_b - girderDepth
Output:       z_bearing_t [m]  Top of bearing level
Note:         girderDepth = 0 for slab bridges without girders.

--- FORMULA PSC-LVL-004 ---
Name:         Bottom of Bearing
Formula:      z_bearing_b = z_bearing_t - bearing_h
Output:       z_bearing_b [m]  Bottom of bearing (= top of pier cap)

--- FORMULA PSC-LVL-005 ---
Name:         Bottom of Pier Cap
Formula:      z_cap_b = z_bearing_b - cap_height
Output:       z_cap_b [m]  Bottom of pier cap / top of pier shaft

--- FORMULA PSC-LVL-006 ---
Name:         Top of Footing
Formula:      z_footing_t = z_soil - (depth - footing_thickness)
Output:       z_footing_t [m]  Top of the foundation footing

--- FORMULA PSC-LVL-007 ---
Name:         Bottom of Footing
Formula:      z_footing_b = z_soil - depth
Output:       z_footing_b [m]  Bottom of foundation

--- FORMULA PSC-LVL-008 ---
Name:         HFL Clearance
Formula:      hflClrnce = z_slab_b - hfl
Output:       hflClrnce [m]  Vertical clearance between soffit and HFL
Standard:     IRS BRules Cl.6 — minimum 0.600m required; 1.5m preferred (freeboard)

--- FORMULA PSC-LVL-009 ---
Name:         OHE (Overhead Electric) Offset
Formula:      ohe_offset = track_gauge / 2 + 2.800
            = 1.676 / 2 + 2.800 = 3.638m
Output:       ohe_offset [m]  Horizontal distance from track CL to OHE mast


SECTION 5: ROAD DECK VARIANT
------------------------------

--- FORMULA PSC-LVL-010 ---
Name:         Slab Top (Road Deck variant)
Formula:      z_slab_t = z_formation   [= formation level directly]
              z_slab_b = z_slab_t - slabD_support
Explanation:
  For road decks, there is no ballast/wearing coat offset. The slab
  top coincides with the formation/road surface.
