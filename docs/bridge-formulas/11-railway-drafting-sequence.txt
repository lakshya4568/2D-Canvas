AAGENTO BRIDGE PLATFORM — DRAFTING PRACTICE
System: Railway bridge drafting sequence (RCC box culvert to multi-span bridge)
Standard References: IRBM-2024, Indian Railways Construction Manual (GAD), RDSO standard drawings, IRS codes
Module: lib/agent/drafter (skills draftsman-method, rcc-box-culvert, rcc-bridge-sequence)
================================================================================

PURPOSE
-------
How a draftsman builds a railway bridge General Arrangement Drawing (GAD) in
CAD: geometry first, annotation last, every size from approved data. A
draftsman does not design the bridge. The drawing is built from approved site,
hydrology, geotechnical and design data and arranged in a fixed sequence so
geometry, levels and notes stay consistent. Indian Railways prefers standard
RDSO drawings wherever they apply.


SECTION 1: WHAT THE DRAFTSMAN DRAWS vs WHAT THE DESIGNER DECIDES
------------------------------------------------------------------

DRF-ROLE-001  The draftsman draws: alignment, axes, outlines, dimensions,
              hatches, notes, sheets; plan, elevation, section and details from
              given values; RDSO standard box/slab details adapted to the site.
DRF-ROLE-002  The designer / competent authority decides: span, waterway,
              loading, member sizes (wall and slab thicknesses), foundation
              type and depth, HFL, scour, bearing capacity, reinforcement,
              material grades, and whether a standard drawing applies.
DRF-ROLE-003  A value the drawing needs but nobody has provided is a REQUIRED
              INPUT: drawn with a placeholder and reported, never invented.
DRF-ROLE-004  For design and construction, IRS Codes override IRBM where they
              conflict.


SECTION 2: LEARNING ORDER (SIMPLE TO COMPLEX)
----------------------------------------------

DRF-SEQ-001   1 single-cell RCC box, square; 2 multi-cell box; 3 skew box;
              4 box with splayed wings, curtain/drop wall, apron; 5 RCC slab
              culvert on abutments; 6 short multi-span RCC slab; 7 RCC T-beam
              with bearings and diaphragms; later PSC girders, steel girders,
              ROB/RUB.
DRF-SEQ-002   The box culvert is the first template because it contains the
              later logic: centre line, clear opening, wall and slab thickness,
              invert, rail level, HFL, wing walls, protection. The missing
              middle skill is keeping axes, levels, thicknesses and dimensions
              associated.


SECTION 3: CAD SETUP BEFORE ANY GEOMETRY
-----------------------------------------

DRF-SET-001   Units millimetres, full size in model space.
DRF-SET-002   Layer standard, linetypes, dimension and text styles, title block
              (project, bridge number, chainage, scale, revision, codes,
              approval boxes).
DRF-SET-003   One vertical datum for every level: rail, formation, HFL, bed,
              invert, soffit, foundation.
DRF-SET-004   Non-plotting construction lines: railway/track centre line,
              direction of increasing kilometrage, transverse axis of the
              structure, upstream/downstream.
DRF-SET-005   Known levels as horizontal construction lines before any
              concrete is drawn.
DRF-SET-006   A GAD shows key plan, chainages, overall dimensions,
              ground/rail/HFL/bed levels, scale, signature boxes,
              codes/manuals/specifications, and bore-log / founding-level data
              (IRCM para 402(1), Table 4.03).


SECTION 4: RCC BOX CULVERT — CONSTRUCTION SEQUENCE
---------------------------------------------------

DRF-BOX-001   Driving values (never guessed): number of cells, clear span and
              clear height per cell, side and interior wall thickness, top and
              bottom slab thickness, haunch size if given, invert/bed level,
              rail, formation, HFL, skew angle, foundation/bedding thickness,
              wing/return wall type and length, ballast cushion below sleeper.
DRF-BOX-002   Controlling axes. Plan: railway centre line, structure centre
              line (square or skewed), upstream and downstream faces, track
              edges / formation width as context. Section/elevation: invert /
              bed, HFL, rail level, formation level, bottom of foundation /
              lean concrete.
DRF-BOX-003   One cell first: the clear opening as a rectangle
              (width = clearSpan, height = clearHeight), then offset outward
              for the concrete:
                outerWidth  = clearSpan + 2 x sideWallThickness
                outerHeight = clearHeight + topSlabThickness + baseSlabThickness
DRF-BOX-004   Multiple cells:
                interiorWalls = cellCount - 1
                outerWidth = cellCount x clearSpan + 2 x sideWallThickness
                             + (cellCount - 1) x interiorWallThickness
              Outer outline minus inner openings equals the walls and slabs;
              never draw a wall as an unrelated rectangle.
DRF-BOX-005   Then, in order: haunches at wall-slab junctions (if designed);
              lean concrete / bedding under the base slab; headwalls / face
              walls at inlet and outlet; curtain wall, drop wall, flooring if
              required; apron, pitching and toe wall upstream and downstream
              (IRBM protection details).
DRF-BOX-006   Wing walls (splayed) or return walls (parallel) after the box is
              stable, symmetric about the bridge centre line unless the
              approved GAD says otherwise; numbered facing increasing
              kilometrage, left/right and upstream/downstream consistently.
DRF-BOX-007   Railway on top only after the box exists: formation, ballast /
              cushion, track centre, rails and sleepers if the sheet needs
              them, parapet / ballast wall, OHE / clearance notes if
              electrified. The structure supports the track, so the box and
              levels come first.
DRF-BOX-008   Dimension order: 1 clear span of each cell, 2 clear height,
              3 wall thicknesses, 4 slab thicknesses, 5 overall outer width and
              height, 6 foundation/bedding thickness, 7 wing-wall lengths and
              splay, 8 chainage and overall length along track, 9 skew angle,
              10 distances from reference points / existing track. A dimension
              never disagrees with the geometry: if the span changes, the
              dimension changes.
DRF-BOX-009   Mandatory level set on a river/nallah GAD: rail, formation, HFL,
              bed/invert, soffit (if shown), bottom of foundation. Also flow
              direction arrows, bore-log / founding note, material grades from
              approved design, RDSO drawing number if a standard box is used,
              loading standard if given in the DBR, revision and approval
              block.
DRF-BOX-010   Hatching and finish: concrete hatch in section, earth/backfill
              behind walls, water/bed in elevation if needed, existing work
              light, proposed work darker. Layouts: key plan, plan, elevation,
              cross section, details at locked scales — several coordinated
              views of the same parameters.
DRF-BOX-011   Worked example (approved data: 3 cells, clearSpan 2000,
              clearHeight 2000, walls 350, top slab 300, base slab 350,
              invert 100.000, rail 104.500, HFL 101.800):
                outerWidth = 3 x 2000 + 4 x 350 = 7400 mm
                soffit     = 100.000 + 2.000 = 102.000
                top of slab = 102.000 + 0.300 = 102.300
              If the clear span becomes 2500 and walls stay 350:
                outerWidth = 3 x 2500 + 4 x 350 = 8900 mm.


SECTION 5: DRAFTING RULES
--------------------------

DRF-STD-001   Separate layers: outline, hidden, centre, dimensions, text,
              hatch, existing, proposed, construction.
DRF-STD-002   Centre lines in centre linetype, never a thick outline.
              Construction lines do not plot.
DRF-STD-003   Existing and proposed work visually distinct.
DRF-STD-004   Title, scale, chainage, bridge number and revision exist before
              issue.
DRF-GEO-001   Clear opening is inside faces, not outer faces.
DRF-GEO-002   Wall thickness is outer face minus inner face, not a separate
              guessed rectangle.
DRF-GEO-003   Interior walls exist only between cells.
DRF-GEO-004   Top slab continuous over cells unless the approved drawing says
              otherwise.
DRF-GEO-005   Invert, soffit and rail level share one datum.
DRF-GEO-006   A box culvert is not placed on a scour-prone bed unless the
              approved design allows it (IRBM).
DRF-GEO-007   A skew structure is marked skew, with both square and skew spans
              given; IRBM advises restricting skew to 30 degrees.


SECTION 6: HYDRAULIC AND CLASSIFICATION RULES THE DRAWING SHOWS
----------------------------------------------------------------
(Show these when they are project data; never invent them. "Requires review".)

DRF-HYD-001   Minor: not major/important. Major: total waterway 18 m or more,
              or one span with clear opening 12 m or more. Important: linear
              waterway 300 m or total waterway 1000 m2 or more, or classified
              important by CBE/CE (IRBM).
DRF-HYD-002   New bridges / rebuilding: minimum clear span 1 m; aim for 1.2 m
              inspection headroom (PCE/CBE may relax).
DRF-HYD-003   Pipe, box and syphon culverts are pressure conduits: the girder
              bridge vertical-clearance table does not apply to them.
DRF-HYD-004   Ordinary bridges (excluding arch, syphon, pipe and box culverts):
              minimum vertical clearance above design flood including afflux
              600 mm for 0-30 cumecs, increasing with discharge (IRBM 312).
DRF-HYD-005   Free board from design flood including afflux to formation / top
              of guide bund generally not less than 1 m; relaxations only by
              PCE/CBE (IRBM 313).


SECTION 7: FOUNDATION RULES THAT AFFECT THE DRAWING
----------------------------------------------------

DRF-FDN-001   Railway bridges normally use open, pile or well foundations.
DRF-FDN-002   Open foundations in ordinary soil: founding depth not less than
              1.75 m below the lowest anticipated scoured bed level. In rock:
              key at least 0.3 m in hard rock, 1.5 m in soft rock. Soft-soil
              rafts need aprons and cut-off / launching aprons.
DRF-FDN-003   RCC box sequence: prepared bed, lean concrete, box, then
              protection works. No deep well foundation under a small box
              unless the approved design says so.


SECTION 8: APPROVAL
--------------------

DRF-APR-001   Open-line railway-bridge GADs need CBE approval.
DRF-APR-002   Construction-organisation GADs of ordinary bridges may be
              approved by CE/Construction, except where waterway is reduced,
              vertical clearance is inadequate, or existing bridges are
              affected (then CBE after DRM/branch clearance).
DRF-APR-003   ROB/RUB/FOB/RFO launching schemes affecting existing lines need
              CBE approval. The draftsman's job is a complete, consistent GAD
              that can be sent for that approval.


SECTION 9: AFTER THE BOX — SLAB, T-BEAM AND MULTI-SPAN BRIDGES
---------------------------------------------------------------

DRF-BRG-001   Order: 1 alignment, chainage, track, flow, levels; 2 abutment and
              pier centre lines (setting-out before outlines); 3 foundations /
              pile caps / wells; 4 abutments, piers, wing walls, returns;
              5 bed blocks and bearings (deck seating depends on them); 6 RCC
              slab or T-beams / girders on the bearings; 7 deck, ballast,
              parapet, drainage (track last); 8 protection works and
              approaches; 9 dimensions, RL/HFL notes, schedules (annotation
              after geometry is stable); 10 title block, GAD checklist, plot.
DRF-BRG-002   RCC/PSC beam-and-slab: in-situ on supports, or launched/precast
              girders with the deck cast later; show seating, diaphragms,
              bearings and the approved erection method if a launching sheet is
              required.
DRF-BRG-003   Weep holes in abutments typically 1 m vertical and 1 m horizontal
              staggered, boulder filling not less than 600 mm, granular backfill
              behind abutments/wing walls; drawn after the wall outline.


SECTION 10: CHECKLIST BEFORE CALLING IT A GAD
----------------------------------------------

DRF-CHK-001   Bridge number, chainage, type, scale, revision exist.
DRF-CHK-002   Direction of increasing kilometrage shown.
DRF-CHK-003   Plan, elevation and section use the same parameters.
DRF-CHK-004   Clear span is inside-to-inside; wall/slab thicknesses match
              offsets, not typed guesses.
DRF-CHK-005   Rail, HFL, bed/invert and foundation levels share one datum.
DRF-CHK-006   Flow direction shown; bore-log / founding reference present if
              required; RDSO/IRS/DBR references written.
DRF-CHK-007   Existing and proposed work separate; no construction line
              plotting; approval block ready.
DRF-CHK-008   Controlling idea: railway context and levels, then the
              box/opening, then thickness, then wings and protection, then
              track, then dimensions and GAD notes.
