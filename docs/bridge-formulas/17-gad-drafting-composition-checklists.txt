PURPOSE
-------
A railway General Arrangement Drawing (GAD) is the definitive legal, contractual, and
engineering record submitted for Chief Bridge Engineer (CBE) / Commissioner of Railway
Safety (CRS) approval. This document formalises sheet composition, pier and girder
numbering conventions, inscription plaques, level markers, flood gauges, and clearance
verification checklists per IRBM-2024 and IRCM Table 4.03.


SECTION 1: SHEET COMPOSITION & COORDINATED VIEWS
--------------------------------------------------

A complete railway bridge GAD comprises coordinated views sharing identical parametric values:
  1. Key Plan / Site Plan: Showing railway alignment, river meander, flow direction arrow,
     approach banks, and nearest railway stations.
  2. General Elevation: Overall longitudinal view showing all spans, piers, abutments,
     rail level, formation, HFL, danger level, bed level, and founding levels.
  3. Longitudinal Section: Cut along the track centre line showing structural depths,
     bed block levels, ballast walls, and approach slabs.
  4. Cross Section(s): Through mid-span and over piers, showing deck slab, ballast cushion,
     girder spacings, diaphragms, bearings, and pier caps.
  5. Foundation Plan: Detailed plan of well curbs, pile caps, or open footings with
     scour envelopes and soil strata logs.
  6. Technical Notes & Title Block: Standards cited, loading class, steel/concrete grades,
     RDSO standard drawing numbers, and approval signature blocks.


SECTION 2: NUMBERING CONVENTIONS (IRBM ANNEXURE 11/5)
------------------------------------------------------

--- FORMULA GAD-NUM-001 ---
Name:         Pier and Abutment Numbering Convention
Rule:         All substructure elements are numbered strictly in the DIRECTION OF
              INCREASING KILOMETRAGE (chainage):
                Abutment 1 (A1)  = Beginning of bridge (lowest chainage)
                Pier 1 (P1)      = First intermediate pier
                Pier 2 (P2)      = Second intermediate pier
                ...
                Abutment 2 (A2)  = Ending of bridge (highest chainage)
              Span 1 is between A1 and P1; Span n is between P(n-1) and A2.

--- FORMULA GAD-GRD-001 ---
Name:         Girder Numbering Convention
Rule:         Facing the direction of increasing kilometrage:
                Girder No. 1 = Left-hand girder
                Girder No. 2 = Second girder from left
                ...
                Girder No. n = Extreme right-hand girder


SECTION 3: INSCRIPTION PLAQUES (IRBM ANNEXURE 11/7)
-----------------------------------------------------

--- FORMULA GAD-PLQ-001 ---
Name:         Foundation Inscription Plaque
Rule:         Every pier and abutment on bridges of span >= 3.0 m must bear an inscription
              plaque (CC or stone slab) recording:
                WL   = Well Foundation
                ON   = Open Foundation
                CP   = Concrete Pile Foundation
                RL-BF = Depth from Rail Level to Bottom of Foundation (m)
                RL-TF = Depth from Rail Level to Top of Foundation (m)
                Strata symbols: S (Sand), C (Clay), R (Rock), M (Moorum)
              Example on pier cap:
                WL
                RL-BF 24.5 m
                RL-TF 8.2 m
                S 12 m, C 6 m, R 6.5 m


SECTION 4: FLOOD GAUGES & DANGER LEVEL MARKINGS (IRBM PARAS 203, 703)
----------------------------------------------------------------------

--- FORMULA GAD-HFL-001 ---
Name:         Highest Flood Level (HFL) & Danger Level Marking
Rule:
  1. HFL Line: Distinct 25 mm broad white line with the year of occurrence painted in
     figures 100 mm deep on the downstream side of abutments/piers.
  2. Danger Level Band: Bright red band 5 cm (50 mm) wide, painted centrally over a white band
     10 cm (100 mm) wide, total length 60 cm (600 mm) on the upstream side.
  3. Danger Level Criteria (IRBM Para 703(4)):
     Danger level is the lowest of:
       (a) Level providing adequate vertical clearance below girder soffit (Table 703(5)(a):
           clearance 150–300 mm for span < 6.1 m, 450 mm for 6.1–12.2 m, 600 mm for 12.2–30.5 m,
           750 mm for 30.5–61 m, 1200 mm for > 61 m).
       (b) Level providing minimum 1.0 m free board to approach banks / guide bunds.
       (c) Level causing afflux > 0.5 m or excessive bed scour.
       (d) For Box Culverts: Danger level = bottom of top slab (soffit).
       (e) For Pipe Culverts: Danger level = top inside of pipe (crown).


SECTION 5: VERTICAL CLEARANCES & FREEBOARD
--------------------------------------------

--- FORMULA GAD-VCL-001 ---
Name:         Vertical Clearance above Design Discharge Q (IRBM Para 312)
Table:
  Discharge Q (cumecs)      Minimum Vertical Clearance (mm)
  --------------------      -------------------------------
  0 to 30                   600 mm
  31 to 300                 600 to 1200 mm (pro-rata)
  301 to 3000               1500 mm
  Above 3000                1800 mm
Exemption:
  Pipe culverts, box culverts, and syphons are designed as pressure conduits;
  no vertical clearance is required (IRBM Para 312(3)).

--- FORMULA GAD-FBD-001 ---
Name:         Free Board to Formation Level (IRBM Para 313)
Formula:      Free Board = FormationLevel - (HFL + Afflux) >= 1.0 m (1000 mm)
              Relaxation (PCE/CBE only):
                Q < 3 cumecs:   min 600 mm
                Q = 3–30 cumecs: min 750 mm
                Q > 30 cumecs:  NO relaxation permitted.
