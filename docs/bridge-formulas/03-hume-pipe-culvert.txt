PURPOSE
-------
A Hume Pipe Culvert uses one or more pre-cast RCC circular pipes placed
below formation level. Each pipe has a nominal span (diameter) and is
surrounded by compacted fill. Used for smaller waterway openings where
an RCC box is over-designed.


SECTION 1: PRIMARY INPUT PARAMETERS
-------------------------------------

Parameter              Symbol            Unit    Description
-----------            ------            ----    -----------
spanSize / pipeDia     spanVal           m       Nominal internal diameter of each pipe
numPipes               numPipes          count   Number of parallel pipes
wallThickness          wallThk           mm      Pipe wall thickness (default 180mm)
formationLevel         fl                m       Formation level above datum
railLevel              rl                m       Rail level above datum
bedLevel               bl                m       Hydraulic bed / invert level above datum
hfl                    hfl               m       Highest Flood Level above datum
floorLevel             floorLevel        m       Floor level of culvert (usually = bl)
foundingDepthBelowScour fd               m       Founding depth below scour (default 1.750m)
formationWidth         formWidth         m       Total formation / road width (default 7.85m)
formationWidthLeft     formLeft          m       Formation width on left side of CL
formationWidthRight    formRight         m       Formation width on right side of CL
pipeSpacing            pipeSpacing       mm      Centre-to-centre spacing between pipes (default 180mm)
beddingThickness       beddingThk        mm      Granular bedding thickness under pipes (default 300mm)
levelingCourse         levelingCourse    mm      Leveling course below bedding (default 150mm)
rubbleSoling           rubbleSoling      mm      Stone soling / rubble layer thickness (default 300mm)
wearingCourseThk       wearCourseThk     mm      Wearing course on approach road (default 100mm)


SECTION 2: GEOMETRIC FORMULAS (HUME PIPE)
-------------------------------------------

--- FORMULA HPC-GEO-001 ---
Name:         Pipe Diameter (in mm)
Formula:      span_mm = spanVal * 1000
Output:       span_mm [mm]  Nominal internal pipe diameter in millimetres

--- FORMULA HPC-GEO-002 ---
Name:         Pipe Wall Thickness Bottom Width Constraint
Formula:      toeWallStemBotW = max(toeWallStemTopW, rawToeWallBotW)
Output:       toeWallStemBotW [mm]  Stem bottom width (never narrower than top)
Explanation:  Structural constraint: a tapered wall cannot be narrower at the
              base than at the top.

--- FORMULA HPC-GEO-003 ---
Name:         Total Culvert External Width
Formula:      total_w = (numPipes * spanVal) + ((numPipes + 1) * (wallThickness / 1000)) + 2.0
Output:       total_w [m]  Overall culvert external width including approach fills
Explanation:
  numPipes pipe barrels each of diameter spanVal, with (numPipes+1) gaps
  at wallThickness spacing, plus 2.0m of additional structural/fill width.

--- FORMULA HPC-GEO-004 ---
Name:         Hydraulic Flow Area
Formula:      flowArea = numPipes * pi * (spanVal / 2)^2
Output:       flowArea [m2]  Total waterway cross-sectional area
Explanation:
  Sum of circular cross-sections of all pipes. Used for waterway adequacy
  checks against design discharge.

--- FORMULA HPC-GEO-005 ---
Name:         Actual Pipe Top Level
Formula:      actualPipeTopM = bl + spanVal + (wallThickness / 1000)
Output:       actualPipeTopM [m]  Elevation of the top of the pipe (outer crown)
Explanation:
  Starting from the bed level (invert / bottom of pipe interior), add the
  internal diameter (spanVal) and one pipe wall thickness to get the
  outer crown elevation.

--- FORMULA HPC-GEO-006 ---
Name:         Earth Cushion Height
Formula:      derivedCushionM = max(0, fl - actualPipeTopM)
Output:       cushionHeight [m]  Compacted fill between pipe crown and formation
Explanation:
  Same concept as RCC box: formation level minus pipe crown gives fill height.
  Clamped to zero if pipe crown is above formation.

--- FORMULA HPC-GEO-007 ---
Name:         Barrel Length
Formula:      barrelLength = formationWidth + 4 * cushionHeight
Output:       barrelLength [m]  Total pipe length including extensions beyond formation
Explanation:
  Each 1m of cushion height requires 2m of additional embankment slope length
  (1:1 slope) on each side, hence the 4× multiplier (2 sides × 2m/m slope).


SECTION 3: LEVEL COMPUTATION FORMULAS
---------------------------------------

--- FORMULA HPC-LVL-001 ---
Name:         Scour Level
Formula:      IF rawScour is provided AND (scour < bl) AND (scour is not legacy default):
                scourLevel = rawScour
              ELSE:
                scourLevel = round((bl - foundingDepthBelowScour) * 1000) / 1000
Output:       scourLevel [m]  Anticipated maximum scour level below bed
Explanation:
  The scour level is the hydraulically computed depth of bed erosion during
  a design flood event. If not explicitly provided, a default of
  1.75m below bed level is used (IRS standard founding depth).
Source:       hume-pipe-rule-engine.ts getComputedScourLevel() lines 109-120

--- FORMULA HPC-LVL-002 ---
Name:         Founding Level
Formula:      foundingLevel = scourLevel - foundingDepthBelowScour
Output:       foundingLevel [m]  Bottom of foundation below scour level
Explanation:
  The actual founding level for the culvert abutment / headwall foundation
  must be foundingDepthBelowScour below the anticipated scour level.
Source:       hume-pipe-rule-engine.ts line 174

--- FORMULA HPC-LVL-003 ---
Name:         Foundation Base Level
Formula:      flBase = bl - foundationThickness        (foundationThickness = 1.219m)
Output:       flBase [m]  Bottom of the total foundation package

--- FORMULA HPC-LVL-004 ---
Name:         Bedding Bottom Level
Formula:      beddingBotM = bl - (beddingThickness / 1000)
Output:       beddingBotM [m]  Bottom of granular bedding under pipes

--- FORMULA HPC-LVL-005 ---
Name:         Leveling Course Bottom Level
Formula:      levelingBotM = beddingBotM - (levelingCourse / 1000)
Output:       levelingBotM [m]  Bottom of leveling course

--- FORMULA HPC-LVL-006 ---
Name:         Found (Rubble Soling) Bottom Level
Formula:      foundBotM = levelingBotM - (rubbleSoling / 1000)
Output:       foundBotM [m]  Bottom of rubble soling / stone foundation layer

--- FORMULA HPC-LVL-007 ---
Name:         Headwall Top Level
Formula:      headwallTopM = bl + (faceWallStemAboveGL / 1000)
Output:       headwallTopM [m]  Top of headwall / face wall above bed level

--- FORMULA HPC-LVL-008 ---
Name:         Rail Level Default
Formula:      rl = fl + 0.762     (if not explicitly provided)
Output:       rl [m]  Rail level
Explanation:  BG standard rail-to-formation offset = 0.762m


SECTION 4: STRUCTURAL HEIGHT FORMULAS
---------------------------------------

--- FORMULA HPC-STR-001 ---
Name:         Total Structural Height
Formula:      total_h = abs(rl - bl)
Output:       total_h [m]  Height from bed level to rail level

--- FORMULA HPC-STR-002 ---
Name:         Abutment Height
Formula:      abutmentHeight = total_h - foundationThickness
Output:       abutmentHeight [m]  Net visible height of the headwall/abutment
Note:         foundationThickness = 1.219m (RDSO standard)

--- FORMULA HPC-STR-003 ---
Name:         Derived Stem Above Ground Level
Formula:      derivedStemAboveGL = round((actualPipeTopM - bl + 0.150) * 1000)
Output:       derivedStemAboveGL [mm]  Face wall stem height above ground line
Explanation:
  The face wall (headwall) stem must rise to cover the top of pipe plus
  a 150mm clearance above.

--- FORMULA HPC-STR-004 ---
Name:         Face Wall Tier 1 Width
Formula:      faceWallTier1Width = faceWallStemBotW + faceWallHeelProj + faceWallToeProj
            = 610 + 500 + 250 = 1360mm (default)
Output:       faceWallTier1Width [mm]  Width of the first foundation tier

--- FORMULA HPC-STR-005 ---
Name:         Face Wall Base Width
Formula:      faceWallBaseWidth = faceWallTier1Width + 2 * faceWallBeddingProj
            = 1360 + 2*200 = 1760mm (default)
Output:       faceWallBaseWidth [mm]  Width at the very base including bedding projections

--- FORMULA HPC-STR-006 ---
Name:         Formation Width (Symmetric Split)
Formula:      IF formLeft not provided: formLeft = rawFormation / 2
              IF formRight not provided: formRight = rawFormation - formLeft
              formationWidth = formLeft + formRight
Output:       formationWidth [m]  Total formation width (same as input or sum)
Explanation:
  If only the total formation width is given, it is split symmetrically.
  If one side is specified, the other takes the remainder.


SECTION 5: DESIGN VALIDATION CHECKS
--------------------------------------

Check ID      Condition                                           Standard
--------      ---------                                           --------
HPC-VAL-001   rl > fl                                             Geometric requirement
HPC-VAL-002   fl > bl                                             Geometric requirement
HPC-VAL-003   hfl <= fl                                           Hydraulic clearance
HPC-VAL-004   flowArea > design discharge / allowable velocity    Waterway adequacy
