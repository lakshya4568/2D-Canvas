PURPOSE
-------
An Open Web Girder (OWG) is a through-truss steel bridge where the train
runs at the bottom chord level (through the truss). Used for long spans
(30.5m, 45.7m, 61.0m, 76.2m, 91.42m, 106.7m clear span).
Standard panel configurations: 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 18 panels.


SECTION 1: RDSO STANDARD PARAMETERS (SPAN-DEPENDENT LOOKUP)
-------------------------------------------------------------

The following parameters are looked up from RDSO standard tables based on span:
  - trussHeight (h)         : Total vertical depth of truss [m]
  - mainGirderCC (cWidth)   : Centre-to-centre distance between main girders [m]
  - trackGauge              : BG track gauge = 1.676m
  - stringerSpacing (strW)  : Spacing of stringers (floor beams) [m]
  - crossGirderDepth (cgDepth) : Depth of cross girders [m]
  - stringerDepth (strDepth): Depth of stringers [m]
  - topChordDepthMm         : Top chord section depth [mm]
  - bottomChordDepthMm      : Bottom chord section depth [mm]
  - endRakerDepthMm         : End raker (inclined end post) depth [mm]
  - verticalsWidthMm        : Vertical member section depth [mm]
  - diagonalsOuterMm        : Outer diagonal member depth [mm]
  - diagonalsMiddleMm       : Middle diagonal member depth [mm]
  - diagonalsInnerMm        : Inner diagonal member depth [mm]

Source: owg-rdso-civil-rules.ts (getRulesForSpan, getMemberDimensionsForSpan)


SECTION 2: TRUSS GEOMETRY FORMULAS
------------------------------------

--- FORMULA OWG-GEO-001 ---
Name:         Panel Length
Formula:      pLen = spanLen / nPanels
Output:       pLen [m]  Length of each truss panel
Inputs:       spanLen = clear span (e.g. 31.926m for 30.5m clear span with bearing offsets)
              nPanels = number of panels (typically 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 18)

--- FORMULA OWG-GEO-002 ---
Name:         Half Girder Width
Formula:      hw = cWidth / 2
Output:       hw [m]  Half the distance between main girder centrelines
Note:         Nodes are placed at y = -hw (left girder) and y = +hw (right girder)

--- FORMULA OWG-GEO-003 ---
Name:         Bearing Gap (span extension beyond truss nodes)
Formula:      gap = 1.06m   (RDSO standard = 1060mm)
Output:       gap [m]  Distance from end node to dirt wall face

--- FORMULA OWG-GEO-004 ---
Name:         Parabolic Camber at Panel Point
Formula:      zCamber = 4 * camber * ratio * (1 - ratio)
Where:        ratio = px / spanLen    (fractional position along span)
              px = i * pLen           (x position of panel point i)
Output:       zCamber [m]  Vertical camber ordinate at panel point i
Explanation:
  A parabolic camber profile is applied to the bottom chord. The formula
  4*c*r*(1-r) gives a parabola with maximum deflection c at mid-span
  (r=0.5) and zero at the supports (r=0 and r=1).


SECTION 3: 14-PANEL AND 18-PANEL SUB-DIVIDED WARREN TRUSS
-----------------------------------------------------------

For 14-panel (91.42m) and 18-panel (106.7m) clear span bridges, the
truss uses a sub-divided Warren configuration:

--- FORMULA OWG-GEO-005 ---
Name:         Half-Height for Sub-Divided Warren
Formula:      halfH = h / 2
              halfPanels = nPanels / 2
Output:       halfH = vertical mid-point of the truss

--- FORMULA OWG-GEO-006 ---
Name:         Top Chord Apex Height (Warren Crown)
Formula:      For panels 0 to halfPanels: apex rises linearly from 0 to halfH
              For panels halfPanels to nPanels: apex falls linearly back to 0
              z_top_chord[i] = halfH - abs(i - halfPanels) * (halfH / halfPanels)
Output:       z_top_chord [m]  Height of top chord at panel i (Warren configuration)


SECTION 4: VERTICAL LEVEL FORMULAS (OWG)
------------------------------------------

--- FORMULA OWG-LVL-001 ---
Name:         Formation Level (Default)
Formula:      formLvl = formation_level   (or 28.120m default)
              IF formLvl is not set: formLvl = 28.120m

--- FORMULA OWG-LVL-002 ---
Name:         HFL Level (Default)
Formula:      hflLvl = hyd.hfl   (or formLvl - 4.0m default)

--- FORMULA OWG-LVL-003 ---
Name:         Bed Level (Default)
Formula:      bedLvl = hyd.bed_lvl   (or formLvl - 12.950m default)
Constraint:   bedLvl must be strictly below hflLvl
