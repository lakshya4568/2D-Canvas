PURPOSE
-------
Bridge foundations transfer structural dead, live, braking, and hydrodynamic loads
safely into deep load-bearing strata while resisting flood scour, tilt, and lateral
displacements. This document formalises the dimensional relationships, rules,
and parametric formulas for Open Foundations, Well Foundations (Caissons),
and Bored Pile Foundations according to Indian Railways standards.


SECTION 1: PRIMARY INPUT PARAMETERS
-------------------------------------

Parameter                  Unit    Description
---------                  ----    -----------
foundations.type           kind    "open" | "well" | "pile" | "raft"
hydraulics.max_scour_level m       Maximum design scour level (RL)
hydraulics.low_water_level m       Lowest recorded dry-season water level (LWL)
hydraulics.bed_level       m       Normal river bed level
hydraulics.lacey_scour_d   m       Lacey's regime scour depth D_lacey = 0.473 * (Q / f)^(1/3)
soil.strata_type           kind    "ordinary_soil" | "hard_rock" | "soft_rock" | "sand" | "clay"
well.shape                 kind    "circular" | "double_d"
well.outer_diameter        mm      External diameter of well steining (D_well)
well.steining_thickness    mm      Thickness of well wall (min 1000 mm)
pile.diameter              mm      Nominal pile diameter (d >= 1000 mm for railway bridges)
pile.count                 count   Number of piles in group under pier/abutment cap


SECTION 2: OPEN FOUNDATIONS (SHALLOW & DEEP)
----------------------------------------------

--- FORMULA FDN-OPN-001 ---
Name:         Open Foundation Depth Below Scour Level (IRBM Para 403)
Formula:      founding_depth_soil >= 1.75 m below lowest anticipated scoured bed level
              founding_depth_hard_rock >= 0.3 m key into sound rock
              founding_depth_soft_rock >= 1.5 m key into soft rock (with horizontal benching)
Output:       foundation_bottom_level [m] = max_scour_level - founding_depth
Rule:         Open foundations must rest on competent strata with adequate safe bearing capacity.
              Sloping rock must be benched horizontally to prevent slip (Annexure 4/4).

--- FORMULA FDN-SHL-001 ---
Name:         Shallow Foundation Protection — Drop Walls & Flooring (IRBM Paras 204, 818)
Formula:      drop_wall_depth = 1.25 * D_lacey  (downstream drop wall, m)
              curtain_wall_depth in [1.5, 2.0] m (upstream cutoff wall)
              flooring_thickness = 300 mm (cement concrete M-20 or stone masonry)
              flooring_extent = full span width between abutments + wing wall protection
Rule:         Shallow foundations (depth < 1.2 m below bed for bridges, < 2.0 m for arches)
              must be protected with full CC/stone flooring and drop walls to prevent undermining.


SECTION 3: WELL FOUNDATIONS (CIRCULAR & DOUBLE-D)
---------------------------------------------------

--- FORMULA FDN-WEL-001 ---
Name:         Well Curb & Cutting Edge Geometry (IRBM Para 418(2), Annexure 4/12)
Formula:      curb_inner_angle = 30°  (up to 45° in sandy strata)
              curb_reinforcement >= 72 kg/m³ (excluding bond rods)
              cutting_edge_material = rolled steel rails / structural angles
              cutting_edge_thickness = fabricated from steel plate 10–20 mm thick
Output:       well_curb_geometry
Explanation:
  The conical curb facilitates displacement of soil into the central dredge hole
  during grabbing.

--- FORMULA FDN-WEL-002 ---
Name:         Well Steining Thickness & Reinforcement (IRBM Para 418(3))
Formula:      steining_thickness >= 1000 mm
              bond_rods_density >= 9 kg/m³ (vertical steel distributed on both faces)
              hoop_reinforcement_density >= 9 kg/m³ (horizontal ring ties)
              cover_to_rods >= 75 mm
Output:       steining_thickness [mm]
Constraint:   Steining must provide sufficient self-weight for sinking without excessive
              kentledge, and resist bursting pressures during sand blows or sudden drops.

--- FORMULA FDN-WEL-003 ---
Name:         Well Hearting, Plugs & Cap Vertical Levels (IRBM Paras 418(4)-(6), 432)
Formula:      bottom_plug_top_y = curb_top_y + 300 mm   (M-15 concrete under water)
              sand_hearting_top_y = steining_top_y - 1750 mm (clean consolidated sand)
              top_plug_thickness = 300 mm               (M-10 concrete)
              well_cap_bottom_y = lwl * 1000 + 300 mm   (300 mm above Low Water Level)
Output:       Z-levels of all well constituent zones
Rule:         The well cap centre is adjusted during casting to match the true pier centre line,
              accommodating any construction shift incurred during sinking.

--- FORMULA FDN-WEL-004 ---
Name:         Permissible Tilt and Shift Limits (IRBM Para 424)
Formula:      max_tilt = 1 in 100   (1.0% of vertical height)
              max_shift = min(D_well / 40, 150 mm)
Rule:         Any shift beyond D/40 or 150 mm requires structural redesign of the well cap
              and foundation bearing pressures before pier construction begins.

--- FORMULA FDN-WEL-005 ---
Name:         Double-D Well Geometry (IRBM Para 417(1))
Formula:      double_d_length <= 2 * double_d_width
              dredge_holes = 2 independent circular/semi-circular openings
              intermediate_cutting_edge_y = outer_cutting_edge_y + 300 mm (prevents rocking)
Output:       Double-D well outline


SECTION 4: BORED PILE FOUNDATIONS
-----------------------------------

--- FORMULA FDN-PIL-001 ---
Name:         Pile Diameter & Minimum Spacing (IRBM Paras 407, 409)
Formula:      pile_diameter d >= 1000 mm  (large diameter bored cast-in-situ piles for railway loads)
              min_spacing_end_bearing = 2.5 * d
              min_spacing_friction    = 3.0 * d
              max_spacing_nominal     = 4.0 * d
Output:       centre-to-centre pile pitch [mm]
Constraint:   Piles deriving capacity from skin friction must maintain >= 3d spacing to avoid
              overlapping stress bulbs and reduced group efficiency.

--- FORMULA FDN-PIL-002 ---
Name:         Pile Cap Embedment & Edge Distance (IRBM Paras 412, 415(12))
Formula:      pile_embedment_in_cap >= 75 mm  (with rebar anchored full development length)
              cap_edge_distance >= d / 2 + 150 mm  (clearance from pile outer face to cap edge)
              concrete_mix >= M-20 (tremie high slump concrete 150–180 mm)
              tremie_pipe_diameter >= 200 mm
Output:       pile_cap_thickness [mm] (typically 1200–2500 mm depending on span reactions)
