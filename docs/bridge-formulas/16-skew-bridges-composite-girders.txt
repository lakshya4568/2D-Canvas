PURPOSE
-------
Road Over Bridges (ROBs) and river/railway crossings frequently intersect the alignment
at an oblique skew angle theta. This document formalises the geometric transformations,
longitudinal girder shifts, diaphragm orientations, bottom lateral bracing layouts,
and end bearing stiffener details from the RDSO 36 m span composite welded girder
standard drawings (RDSO/B-11778/14 for theta <= 20°, RDSO/B-11778/15 for 20° < theta <= 30°).


SECTION 1: PRIMARY INPUT PARAMETERS
-------------------------------------

Parameter                  Unit    Description
---------                  ----    -----------
skew.angle_theta           deg     Skew angle between bridge centre line and normal to pier/abutment
girder.square_span         mm      Square span perpendicular to supports (L_sq)
girder.spacing             mm      Centre-to-centre lateral spacing between steel I-girders (W_g)
girder.count               count   Number of parallel longitudinal steel girders (typically 2 to 4)
girder.depth               mm      Overall depth of steel I-girder web + flanges
bearing.seat_type          enum    "square_to_girder" | "along_skew"


SECTION 2: SKEW SPAN & LONGITUDINAL GIRDER SHIFT
--------------------------------------------------

--- FORMULA SKW-GEO-001 ---
Name:         Skew Span along Track / Road Centre Line
Formula:      L_skew = L_sq / cos(theta)
Parameters:
  L_sq  = Square span measured perpendicular to pier/abutment faces
  theta = Skew angle in degrees
Output:       L_skew [mm]  Longitudinal span distance between support centre lines

--- FORMULA SKW-SFT-001 ---
Name:         Longitudinal Girder Shift (RDSO/B-11778/14 Note 5)
Formula:      S_shift = W_g * tan(theta)
Parameters:
  W_g   = Transverse centre-to-centre spacing between adjacent girders
  theta = Skew angle
Output:       S_shift [mm]  Longitudinal displacement between ends of adjacent girders
Explanation:
  Because supports lie along a line skewed at theta, adjacent girders supported on the
  same abutment cap have their bearing centre lines shifted longitudinally by S_shift.
  The total bridge footprint forms a parallelogram in plan.


SECTION 3: DIAPHRAGM ORIENTATIONS (CRITICAL CODE DISTINCTION)
--------------------------------------------------------------

--- FORMULA SKW-DPH-001 ---
Name:         End Diaphragm vs Intermediate Diaphragm Orientation (RDSO/B-11778/14 Note 4)
Rule:
  1. END DIAPHRAGMS are detailed ALONG THE SKEW line over bearing supports:
       L_end_diaphragm = W_g / cos(theta)
  2. INTERMEDIATE DIAPHRAGMS are strictly PERPENDICULAR (90°) to the longitudinal girders:
       L_int_diaphragm = W_g
Explanation:
  Orienting intermediate cross-frames square to girders eliminates severe torsional warping
  and out-of-plane web bending under heavy live wheel loads.
  Only the end diaphragm at the support face spans along the skew.


SECTION 4: BOTTOM LATERAL BRACING & GUSSET PLATES
---------------------------------------------------

--- FORMULA SKW-BRC-001 ---
Name:         Bottom Lateral Bracing Geometry (RDSO/B-11778/14 Note 5)
Rule:
  - Lateral bracing intersection points on each girder remain at the same panel
    locations as in a square span.
  - Because one girder is shifted longitudinally by S_shift = W_g * tan(theta),
    the cross-panel diagonal bracing lengths become asymmetric:
      L_brace_1 = sqrt(panel_length - S_shift)^2 + W_g^2)
      L_brace_2 = sqrt(panel_length + S_shift)^2 + W_g^2)
  - Gusset plates at the acute and obtuse girder ends are BENT for theta > 20°
    (RDSO/B-11778/15) to accommodate the oblique angle without edge prying.

--- FORMULA SKW-STF-001 ---
Name:         End Bearing Stiffeners & Brackets
Formula:      X_width = (30 * Card * theta) / Sn + (theta / 2)  [mm]
              L1_outer = outer edge distance to end stiffeners
Output:       End stiffener plate bevel and weld bevel geometry.


SECTION 5: SKEW DECK SLAB REINFORCEMENT & ACUTE CORNERS
---------------------------------------------------------

--- FORMULA SKW-SLB-001 ---
Name:         Skew Deck Slab Detailing (RDSO/B-11778/14 Fig. 5)
Rule:
  - For theta <= 20°: Main transverse slab reinforcement runs parallel to skew supports.
  - For theta > 20°: Main transverse slab reinforcement runs PERPENDICULAR to main girders;
    the acute triangular corner slabs at both ends receive extra fan-shaped top and bottom
    reinforcement grids to resist high torsional and uplifting moments.
