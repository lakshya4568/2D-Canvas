PURPOSE
-------
Substructures transmit vertical and lateral loads (dead load, train live load,
braking/tractive force, wind, seismic, centrifugal force, hydrodynamic flow, and
earth pressure) from the bridge superstructure to the foundation. This document
formalises the geometry, batter ratios, clearances, drainage, and backfill rules
governing piers, abutments, wing walls, return walls, and bed blocks according
to the Indian Railways Bridge Manual (IRBM-2024).


SECTION 1: PRIMARY INPUT PARAMETERS
-------------------------------------

Parameter                  Unit    Description
---------                  ----    -----------
pier.height                mm      Total height of pier shaft from foundation/well cap to pier cap
pier.top_width             mm      Width of pier top along bridge transverse axis
pier.top_thickness         mm      Thickness of pier top along bridge longitudinal axis
pier.batter_ratio          ratio   Transverse batter ratio (1 in 12 to 1 in 24)
abutment.height            mm      Height of abutment stem from foundation top to bed block
abutment.front_batter      ratio   Front face batter ratio (1 in 10 to 1 in 16)
abutment.ballast_wall_h    mm      Height of ballast wall above bed block
abutment.ballast_wall_t    mm      Thickness of ballast wall (typically 450–600 mm)
wing.splay_angle           deg     Splay angle of wing walls relative to track axis (typically 30° to 45°)
wing.embankment_slope      ratio   Slope of approach embankment (typically 1.5:1 or 2:1, H:V)
backfill.boulder_thickness mm      Thickness of hand-packed boulder backing behind wall (min 600 mm)
bed_block.thickness        mm      Depth of in-situ RCC through bed block (min 300–450 mm)
bearing.pedestal_spacing   mm      Centre-to-centre distance between girder bearings


SECTION 2: PIER GEOMETRY & BATTER FORMULAS
--------------------------------------------

--- FORMULA SUB-BAT-001 ---
Name:         Masonry / Mass Concrete Pier Batter
Formula:      batter_H = 1
              batter_V in [12, 24]   (i.e. 1 in 12 to 1 in 24, IRBM Para 604(1)(b))
              pier_base_thickness = pier_top_thickness + 2 * (pier_height / batter_V)
Output:       pier_base_thickness [mm]  Longitudinal thickness at bottom of pier shaft
Explanation:
  Piers taper upwards to reduce self-weight and material while widening at the base
  for flexural moment capacity. The transverse batter is symmetric about the pier
  vertical axis.

--- FORMULA SUB-BAT-002 ---
Name:         Pier Top Width along Transverse Axis
Formula:      pier_top_width = bearing_spacing + 2 * bearing_width + 2 * edge_clearance
Output:       pier_top_width [mm]
Constraint:   edge_clearance >= 250 mm to resist diagonal shear under bearing reaction
              (IRBM Para 604(1)(b), BS-118 Clause 14.3).

--- FORMULA SUB-BAT-003 ---
Name:         Pier Types (Annexure 6/1)
Description:
  1. Mass Concrete Pier: Solid rectangular or rounded ends, battered 1:12 to 1:24.
  2. RCC Framed Type Pier: Two or more columns with tie beams and top capping beam.
  3. Hammerhead Pier: Slender vertical shaft with cantilevered pier cap supporting bearings.
  4. Cellular Type Pier: Hollow concrete box sections with diaphragms for tall viaducts (>30 m).


SECTION 3: ABUTMENT GEOMETRY FORMULAS
---------------------------------------

--- FORMULA SUB-ABT-001 ---
Name:         Abutment Front Batter & Base Width
Formula:      front_batter_V in [10, 16]   (1 in 10 to 1 in 16, IRBM Para 604(1)(c))
              front_spread = abutment_height / front_batter_V
              base_width >= 0.4 * abutment_height   (Gravity stability rule)
              rear_profile = stepped or battered to keep resultant within middle third/three-fourths
Output:       abutment_base_width [mm]

--- FORMULA SUB-ABT-002 ---
Name:         Ballast Wall (Back Wall) Dimensions
Formula:      ballast_wall_top_y = formation_level * 1000 - sleeper_thickness
              ballast_wall_height = ballast_wall_top_y - bed_block_top_y
              ballast_wall_thickness in [450, 600] mm
Output:       ballast_wall_height [mm], ballast_wall_thickness [mm]
Explanation:
  The ballast wall retains approach embankment ballast and prevents it from falling
  onto the bearing shelf and bridge seats.

--- FORMULA SUB-ABT-003 ---
Name:         Abutment Types (Annexure 6/2)
Description:
  1. Mass Concrete / Gravity Abutment: Front batter with stepped rear face.
  2. Buried / Spill-Through Abutment: Earth slope spills forward around columns where
     waterway is non-critical.
  3. Box Return (U-Abutment): Monolithic return walls extending straight back.
  4. Counterfort Abutment: Thin reinforced wall slab with vertical triangular ribs.


SECTION 4: WING WALLS & RETURN WALLS FORMULAS
-----------------------------------------------

--- FORMULA SUB-WNG-001 ---
Name:         Splayed Wing Wall Geometry & Length
Formula:      delta_H = formation_level - bed_level    (in metres)
              embankment_run = delta_H * embankment_slope_H
              wing_length = embankment_run / cos(splay_angle)
Output:       wing_length [mm]
Explanation:
  Splayed wing walls guide river flow smoothly into the bridge opening while
  retaining the 1.5:1 or 2:1 approach bank slopes. The top coping follows the slope
  down to the natural ground level or bed level.

--- FORMULA SUB-RET-001 ---
Name:         Parallel Return Wall Length & Stepping
Formula:      return_length = delta_H * embankment_slope_H
              coping_slope = 1 / embankment_slope_H
              stepped_footing_levels = stepped at 1 m to 1.5 m intervals to suit rock/soil strata
Output:       return_length [mm]
Explanation:
  Return walls run parallel to the railway track. They are preferred on high banks
  or where right-of-way is restricted (IRBM Para 604(2)(b)). Butt joints must be
  provided between wing walls and abutments to accommodate differential settlement.


SECTION 5: DRAINAGE, WEEP HOLES & BACKFILL
--------------------------------------------

--- FORMULA SUB-DRN-001 ---
Name:         Weep Hole Grid Layout (IRBM Paras 207, 605)
Formula:      weep_pitch_x = 1000 mm   (horizontal spacing)
              weep_pitch_y = 1000 mm   (vertical spacing, staggered)
              weep_dia     = 100 mm (or 75 mm min)
              weep_min_y   = max(bed_level * 1000 + 250, lwl * 1000, fsl * 1000)
Output:       Weep hole array coordinates
Rule:         The lowest row of weep holes must be at least 250 mm above normal bed
              level, above low water level (LWL) in streams, and above full supply level (FSL) in canals.

--- FORMULA SUB-BCK-001 ---
Name:         Hand-Packed Boulder Backing (IRBM Para 605(3), Annexure 6/3)
Formula:      boulder_thickness >= 600 mm for the full height of abutments and wing walls.
              backfill_filter = granular material of GW, GP, SW groups (IS:1498), free of clay.
Output:       boulder_thickness [mm]
Rule:         Weep holes must be backed with hand-packed boulders to prevent fine backfill
              particles from washing through and choking drainage paths.


SECTION 6: BED BLOCKS & BEARINGS
----------------------------------

--- FORMULA SUB-BLK-001 ---
Name:         Through RCC Bed Block Dimensions (IRBM Paras 221, 514)
Formula:      bed_block_thickness in [300, 600] mm (typically 450 mm for BG bridges)
              bed_block_width = pier_top_width
              bed_block_length = pier_top_thickness
              concrete_grade >= M-25
Output:       bed_block_thickness [mm]
Rule:         Independent isolated bed blocks are prone to shaking under dynamic train
              impact. Indian Railways requires continuous through RCC bed blocks under all
              bearings to prevent cracking and distribute reaction evenly into the substructure.
