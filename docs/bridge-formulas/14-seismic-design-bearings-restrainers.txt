PURPOSE
-------
Earthquake ground vibrations induce horizontal and vertical dynamic inertia forces on
railway bridge superstructures, bearings, piers, abutments, and foundations. This document
formalises the design equations, seismic zone factors, soil spectrum coefficients,
minimum bearing seating widths (shelf dimensions), and restraint detailing prescribed
by RDSO Guidelines on Seismic Design of Railway Bridges (BS-118, 2015).

SECTION 2: SEISMIC COEFFICIENTS & SCOPE EXEMPTIONS
----------------------------------------------------

--- FORMULA SEI-COE-001 ---
Name:         Elastic Seismic Acceleration Coefficient (BS-118 Clause 7.1)
Formula:      A_h = (Z / 2) * I * (Sa_g)
Parameters:
  Z (Zone factor, Table 3):
    Zone II = 0.10, Zone III = 0.16, Zone IV = 0.24, Zone V = 0.36
    Near fault (< 10 km from active fault): Z_effective = 1.20 * Z
  I (Importance factor, Table 4):
    Category I = 1.5  (Important bridges irrespective of route; Major bridges on Group A, B, C)
    Category II = 1.25 (Major bridges on other routes; Other bridges on Group A, B, C)
    Other bridges = 1.0
  Sa_g (Response Spectrum Acceleration Coefficient, 5% damping):
    Type I (Rock, N > 30):
      Sa_g = 2.50 (T < 0.40s), 1.00 / T (0.40 <= T <= 3.00s), 0.33 (T > 3.00s)
    Type II (Medium, 10 <= N <= 30):
      Sa_g = 2.50 (T < 0.55s), 1.36 / T (0.55 <= T <= 3.00s), 0.45 (T > 3.00s)
    Type III (Soft, N < 10):
      Sa_g = 2.50 (T < 0.67s), 1.67 / T (0.67 <= T <= 3.00s), 0.56 (T > 3.00s)
Output:       A_h [-]  Horizontal seismic design acceleration coefficient

--- FORMULA SEI-COE-002 ---
Name:         Culvert & Small Bridge Seismic Analysis Exemption (BS-118 Clauses 4.4, 4.5)
Rule:
  1. Box culverts and pipe culverts NEED NOT be analysed for seismic forces (Clause 4.4).
  2. In Zones II & III, bridges with overall length < 60 m or spans < 15 m need not be analysed (Clause 4.5(a)).
  3. Single span bridges up to 30 m span need not be analysed (Clause 4.5(b)).
  CRITICAL: Even when analysis is exempted, bridges MUST provide:
    (i) Minimum seating width W (Clause 14.3)
    (ii) Connections designed for elastic seismic force from superstructure.


SECTION 3: MINIMUM SEATING WIDTH AT SUPPORTS
----------------------------------------------

--- FORMULA SEI-SEAT-001 ---
Name:         Minimum Seating Width at Supports (BS-118 Clause 14.3)
Formula:      Zones II and III:
                W_min = 300 + 1.5 * L + 6.0 * Hp   (mm)
              Zones IV and V:
                W_min = 500 + 2.5 * L + 10.0 * Hp  (mm)
Parameters:
  L  = Length in metres of superstructure to the adjacent expansion joint
  Hp = Height in metres of column or pier.
       For abutments: Hp is average height of all columns to next joint;
       for single span bridges: Hp = 0.
Output:       W_min [mm]  Minimum bearing shelf seating dimension normal to face of support
Explanation:
  Prevents span unseating and catastrophic span loss during severe earthquake ground shaking.
  Measured from the closest end of the girder to the edge of the pier cap/abutment seat.


SECTION 4: BEARINGS, RESTRAINERS & EXPANSION GAPS
---------------------------------------------------

--- FORMULA SEI-REST-001 ---
Name:         Seismic Restrainers & Reaction Blocks (BS-118 Clause 11.3.2.4)
Formula:      F_restrainer = 1.5 * A_h * W_trib
Parameters:
  W_trib = Tributary weight of superstructure supported at that pier/abutment
  A_h    = Elastic horizontal seismic coefficient
Output:       F_restrainer [kN]  Design horizontal capacity of concrete seismic stopper / shear key
Rule:         Reaction blocks restrain lateral and longitudinal dislodgement of girders if
              bearings shear or walk under extreme dynamic cyclic displacement.

--- FORMULA SEI-SEP-001 ---
Name:         Separation Clearance at Expansion Joints (BS-118 Clause 14.2.1)
Formula:      clearance_joint >= sqrt(Delta_1^2 + Delta_2^2)
Parameters:
  Delta_1, Delta_2 = Elastic lateral/longitudinal displacements of the two adjacent units
Output:       clearance_joint [mm]
Explanation:
  Prevents adjacent bridge decks from pounding against one another during out-of-phase shaking.

--- FORMULA SEI-BEAR-001 ---
Name:         Elastomeric Bearing Seismic Shear Strain Limits (BS-118 Clause 17.7.2)
Formula:      gamma_c <= 2.5                   (compression strain)
              gamma_c + gamma_ss + gamma_r <= 5.0 (non-seismic combination)
              gamma_c + gamma_seq + 0.5 * gamma_r <= 5.5 (seismic combination)
Parameters:
  gamma_c   = 3 * S1 * P / (2 * Ar * G * (1 + 2 * k * S1^2))
  gamma_seq = d_i / Tr  (seismic lateral displacement divided by total elastomer thickness)
  gamma_r   = B^2 * theta / (2 * t_i * Tr)  (rotation strain)


SECTION 5: HYDRODYNAMIC & DYNAMIC EARTH PRESSURE
--------------------------------------------------

--- FORMULA SEI-HYD-001 ---
Name:         Hydrodynamic Force on Submerged Pier (BS-118 Clause 12.2)
Formula:      F_hydro = Ce * A_h * W_e
Parameters:
  Ce  = Submergence coefficient (Table 8, 0.39 to 0.73 based on H / radius)
  W_e = Weight of water in a hypothetical enveloping cylinder circumscribing the pier
Output:       F_hydro [kN]  Horizontal force applied at 3/7 of submerged depth from river bed

--- FORMULA SEI-EAR-001 ---
Name:         Dynamic Earth Pressure on Abutments (Mononobe-Okabe, BS-118 Appendix F)
Formula:      E_AE = 0.5 * gamma * H^2 * (1 +- A_v) * K_AE
              theta = atan(A_h / (1 +- A_v))
              A_v = (2 / 3) * A_h
Rule:         Dynamic earth pressure increment is applied at mid-height (0.5 H) of the
              abutment wall, while static active earth pressure is applied at H / 3.
