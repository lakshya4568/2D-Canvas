PURPOSE
-------
River training and protective works confine and guide alluvial stream flows smoothly
through the bridge waterway opening, prevent outflanking of approach banks, and protect
piers, abutments, and guide bunds from deep local scour holes. This document formalises
the geometric parameters, curved mole head dimensions, launching apron rules, and spur
layouts per the Indian Railways Bridge Manual (IRBM-2024, Chapter VIII).


SECTION 1: PRIMARY INPUT PARAMETERS
-------------------------------------

Parameter                  Unit    Description
---------                  ----    -----------
hydraulics.discharge_Q     cumecs  Design flood discharge Q (50-year recurrence)
hydraulics.waterway_width  m       Regime linear waterway width L = 4.75 * sqrt(Q) (Lacey)
hydraulics.max_scour_depth m       Maximum anticipated scour depth D_scour below HFL
hydraulics.low_water_level m       Low water level (dry season bed level)
pitching.slope_thickness   m       Thickness of stone pitching on bank/bund slope (T, typ 0.3 m)
spur.length                m       Projection length of spur / groyne into channel


SECTION 2: GUIDE BUND (BELL'S BUND) GEOMETRY
----------------------------------------------

--- FORMULA RTW-GB-001 ---
Name:         Guide Bund Shank Lengths (IRBM Para 810(2)(b))
Formula:      upstream_shank_length   = 1.0 * L to 1.5 * L
              downstream_tail_length  = 0.25 * L to 0.4 * L
Parameters:
  L = Linear waterway width of bridge between abutments (m)
Output:       L_us [m], L_ds [m]
Explanation:
  The upstream shank guides flood waters into a uniform parallel flow without
  excessive obliquity or eddy formation. The downstream shank provides a gentle exit
  without vertical whirlpools.

--- FORMULA RTW-GB-002 ---
Name:         Curved Upstream Mole Head Geometry (IRBM Para 810(2)(e))
Formula:      R_up = clamp(0.45 * L, 150 m, 600 m)
              sweep_angle_up in [120°, 145°]
              R_down = clamp(0.35 * R_up, 50 m, 300 m)
              sweep_angle_down in [45°, 60°]
Output:       R_up [m], sweep_angle_up [deg]
Explanation:
  The mole head bears the brunt of flood attack. The continuous circular curve
  prevents flow separation and stagnation shoals along the outer flank.

--- FORMULA RTW-GB-003 ---
Name:         Guide Bund Cross-Section (IRBM Para 810(2)(f), 810(2)(g))
Formula:      top_width in [6.0, 9.0] m   (permits maintenance trucks and boulder stacking)
              side_slope = 2:1             (2 horizontal to 1 vertical)
              free_board >= 1.0 m          (above design HFL including afflux and wave head)
              stone_pitching_thickness = 250 to 300 mm (man-size boulders 35–55 kg)
              filter_layer_thickness   = 200 to 300 mm (graded gravel/sand filter below stone)


SECTION 3: LAUNCHING APRONS
-----------------------------

--- FORMULA RTW-APR-001 ---
Name:         Launching Apron Thickness & Width (IRBM Para 810(3))
Formula:      apron_width = 1.5 * (D_scour - low_water_level)
              apron_thickness_straight = 1.5 * T
              apron_thickness_curved_inner = 1.5 * T
              apron_thickness_curved_outer = 2.25 * T
Parameters:
  T = Pitching thickness on slope (typically 0.30 m)
  D_scour = Deepest anticipated scour level below low water level
Output:       apron_width [m], apron_thickness [m]
Rule:         Aprons are laid flat on the dry bed at the lowest feasible level. When flood
              scour undermines the riverbed, the stone apron launches automatically down the
              scoured slope at the natural angle of repose (1.5:1 to 2:1), self-armouring the bank.


SECTION 4: SPURS (GROYNES)
----------------------------

--- FORMULA RTW-SPUR-001 ---
Name:         Spur Orientation, Types & Spacing (IRBM Para 811)
Classification:
  1. Repelling (Deflecting) Spurs: Incline UPSTREAM at 60° to 70° to flow. Deflects current
     away from bank; forms a still-water silting pocket on the upstream flank.
  2. Attracting Spurs: Incline downstream; pulls flow toward nose.
  3. Normal (Sedimenting) Spurs: Set perpendicular (90°) to bank; promotes silting between spurs.
Formula:      spur_spacing = 2.0 * spur_length to 2.5 * spur_length
              spur_projection = 0.11 * channel_width to 0.15 * channel_width
              mole_head_apron_scour = 2.5 * D_lacey to 2.75 * D_lacey
              free_board = 1.0 m above HFL


SECTION 5: BOULDER CRATES & SAUSAGE CRATES
--------------------------------------------

--- FORMULA RTW-CRT-001 ---
Name:         Boulder Size for Water Velocity (IRC:89, IRBM Para 815)
Formula:      V = 4.893 * sqrt(d)  ==>  d_min = (V / 4.893)^2
Parameters:
  V     = Mean design flood velocity in m/s
  d_min = Equivalent diameter of stone in metres (assumed spherical, specific gravity 2.65)
Output:       d_min [m]  Minimum boulder diameter to resist washing away
Rule:         Where water velocity demands stone larger than can be handled manually (>55 kg),
              GI wire crates (4 SWG or 6 SWG wire) filled with smaller stones are used.

--- FORMULA RTW-CRT-002 ---
Name:         Sausage Crates (IRBM Para 816)
Formula:      sausage_diameter in [600, 900] mm (flexible cylindrical wire netting filled with stones)
Output:       Cylindrical flexible slope protection units.


SECTION 6: FLOORING & DROP WALLS (MINOR BRIDGES)
--------------------------------------------------

--- FORMULA RTW-FLR-001 ---
Name:         Flooring, Curtain & Drop Wall Depth (IRBM Paras 204, 818)
Formula:      drop_wall_depth_ds = 1.25 * D_lacey   (downstream drop wall)
              curtain_wall_depth_us = 1.0 * D_lacey (upstream curtain wall)
              flooring_slope = bed_slope             (matches natural stream gradient)
Rule:         Floor must cover the entire width and length of abutments including wing walls.
              Top of drop wall matches stream bed slope to prevent waterfall plunge scour.
