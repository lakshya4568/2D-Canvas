PURPOSE
-------
A Reinforced Cement Concrete (RCC) Box Culvert is a closed rectangular
cross-section hydraulic structure placed below railway formation level.
Water flows through the barrel opening(s). The culvert supports train
load (live load) transmitted through the formation and earth cushion.


SECTION 1: PRIMARY INPUT PARAMETERS
-------------------------------------

Parameter        Symbol    Unit    Description
-----------      ------    ----    -----------
cells            n         count   Number of parallel barrel openings (minimum 1)
clear_w          cW        m       Clear width of each barrel opening (horizontal)
clear_h          cH        m       Clear height of each barrel opening (vertical)
t_wall_o         tOW       m       Outer wall thickness (the two end walls)
t_wall_i         tIW       m       Inner wall (partition) thickness between barrels
t_slab_t         tST       m       Top slab thickness
t_slab_b         tSB       m       Bottom slab thickness
haunch_size      haunch    m       Haunch chamfer size at interior corners (both directions equal)
wearing_course   wearC     m       Wearing course / ballast protection thickness inside barrel
bed_lvl          bedLvl    m       Hydraulic bed level (finished floor / invert level) in m above datum
formation_lvl    formLvl   m       Proposed formation level (top of embankment) in m above datum
rail_level       railLvl   m       Proposed rail level in m above datum
pcc_sub_base     pccB      m       Plain Cement Concrete sub-base (under-box) thickness
sand_fill_depth  sandFill  m       Granular fill / sand bedding depth below PCC (also: found_d or foundation layers)
hfl              hfl       m       Highest Flood Level in m above datum
soffit_level     soffitLvl m       Top of water face (underside of top slab) in m above datum
wing_l           wingL     m       Wing wall length (from front face of box)
apron_l          apronL    m       Apron / flexible apron length downstream


SECTION 2: GEOMETRIC FORMULAS (RCC BOX RAILWAY)
------------------------------------------------

--- FORMULA RCR-GEO-001 ---
Name:         Total Box Width
Formula:      total_w = (cW * cells) + (tOW * 2) + (tIW * (cells - 1))
Output:       total_w    [m]   Full external width of the culvert box
Explanation:
  The culvert consists of n=cells barrel openings each cW wide, separated by
  n-1 inner partition walls of thickness tIW, and bounded by 2 outer walls
  of thickness tOW. Adding all widths gives the external footprint width.
Example:      2 cells * 5.0m + 2*0.35m + 1*0.35m = 11.05m
Source:       rcc-box-rule-engine.ts line 33
Constraint:   cells >= 1, cW > 0, tOW >= 0.3m (IRS minimum), tIW >= 0.3m

--- FORMULA RCR-GEO-002 ---
Name:         Total Box Height
Formula:      total_h = cH + tST + tSB
Output:       total_h    [m]   Full external height of the culvert box
Explanation:
  External height is the clear internal height cH plus the top slab thickness
  tST plus the bottom slab thickness tSB.
Source:       rcc-box-rule-engine.ts line 34

--- FORMULA RCR-GEO-003 ---
Name:         Hydraulic Flow Area
Formula:      flow_area = cW * cH * cells
Output:       flow_area  [m2]  Total waterway area available for flow
Explanation:
  Sum of all barrel cross-sectional areas available for water passage.
  Used for hydraulic design and waterway adequacy checks.
Source:       rcc-box-rule-engine.ts line 35
Constraint:   flow_area must exceed the design discharge requirement (Q)

--- FORMULA RCR-GEO-004 ---
Name:         Structural Top-of-Bottom-Slab Level
Formula:      structTopBotSlab = bedLvl - wearC
Output:       structTopBotSlab [m] Elevation of the top face of the bottom slab
Explanation:
  The bed level (bedLvl) is the finished floor level (top of wearing course).
  Subtracting the wearing course thickness gives the structural datum of the
  bottom slab's top surface.
Source:       rcc-box-rule-engine.ts line 39

--- FORMULA RCR-GEO-005 ---
Name:         Bottom of Box / Bottom of Bottom Slab Level
Formula:      z_bot = structTopBotSlab - tSB
Output:       z_bot  [m]  Elevation of the bottom face of the bottom slab
Explanation:
  The underside of the bottom slab (founding level of the box structure).
  z_bot = bedLvl - wearC - tSB
Source:       rcc-box-rule-engine.ts line 40

--- FORMULA RCR-GEO-006 ---
Name:         Top of Box Level
Formula:      z_top = z_bot + total_h
Output:       z_top  [m]  Elevation of the top face of the top slab
Equivalent:   z_top = bedLvl - wearC - tSB + cH + tST + tSB
            = bedLvl - wearC + cH + tST
Explanation:
  The top of the box structure. This is also called rccBoxTopLvl.
Source:       rcc-box-rule-engine.ts line 41

--- FORMULA RCR-GEO-007 ---
Name:         Soffit Level (Underside of Top Slab)
Formula:      soffit = z_top - tST
Output:       soffit [m]  Elevation of the underside of the top slab
Explanation:
  The soffit is the bottom surface of the top slab, which is the hydraulic
  crown of the culvert. Water must stay below this level for the culvert
  to function as a free-surface channel.
Source:       rcc-box-rule-engine.ts line 42
Validation:   soffit must be >= hfl (Highest Flood Level)


SECTION 3: LEVEL COMPUTATION FORMULAS
--------------------------------------

--- FORMULA RCR-LVL-001 ---
Name:         RCC Box Top Level
Formula:      rccBoxTopLvl = structTopBotSlab + cH + tST
            = bedLvl - wearC + cH + tST
Output:       rccBoxTopLvl [m]  Top of slab elevation
Explanation:
  The upper surface of the top slab. The earth cushion, formation, and rail
  levels all sit above this datum.
Source:       rcc-box-rule-engine.ts line 46

--- FORMULA RCR-LVL-002 ---
Name:         Earth Cushion Thickness
Formula:      earthCushion = max(formLvl - rccBoxTopLvl, 0)
Output:       earthCushion [m]  Thickness of fill between top of box and formation
Explanation:
  The formation level minus the top of the box gives the earth cushion depth.
  Per IRS, a minimum of 75mm (0.075m) is required over RCC box culverts for
  railway track. If the result is negative (formLvl below top of box),
  the cushion is clamped to zero.
Source:       rcc-box-rule-engine.ts line 48
              rcc-box-half-section-view.ts line 111
Constraint:   earthCushion >= 0.075m (IRS requirement)
Note:         If earthCushion = 0, the 225mm parapet gap line and cushion
              callouts are suppressed in the drawing.

--- FORMULA RCR-LVL-003 ---
Name:         PCC Top Level
Formula:      pccTop = z_bot   (identical — top of PCC is the underside of the bottom slab)
Output:       pccTop [m]

--- FORMULA RCR-LVL-004 ---
Name:         PCC Bottom Level
Formula:      pccBot = z_bot - pccB
Output:       pccBot [m]  Underside of Plain Cement Concrete sub-base

--- FORMULA RCR-LVL-005 ---
Name:         Granular Fill Bottom Level
Formula:      granBot = pccBot - sandFill
Output:       granBot [m]  Bottom of the granular fill / sand cushion layer
Explanation:
  The founding stratum / excavation bottom. All fill layers are stacked
  upward from granBot to pccBot.

--- FORMULA RCR-LVL-006 ---
Name:         Rail Level (Derived Default)
Formula:      railLvl = formLvl + RAIL_FORMATION_OFFSET
Output:       railLvl [m]  Elevation of top of rail
Note:         RAIL_FORMATION_OFFSET is a project constant (typically 0.762m for BG).
              If rail_level is explicitly provided by the user, that value is used directly.

--- FORMULA RCR-LVL-007 ---
Name:         Span Length (Barrel Barrel Length / Drawing Extent)
Formula:      spanLen = (formation.left + formation.right) + 4 * earthCushion
Output:       spanLen [m]  Total span length of culvert including approach fill
Explanation:
  The formation extents (left bank + right bank widths) represent the roadbed
  width. Adding 4 times the earth cushion accounts for the 1:1 embankment
  slope on both sides that extends beyond the box.
Source:       rcc-box-rule-engine.ts line 50


SECTION 4: SCREEN / DRAWING COORDINATE FORMULAS
-------------------------------------------------

--- FORMULA RCR-DRAW-001 ---
Name:         Scale Factor (sf)
Formula:      sf = scale / 40.0
Output:       sf  [px/m * normalized]  Drawing scale factor for line weights and text
Note:         40.0 is the reference scale denominator. At 1:40 scale, sf = 1.0.

--- FORMULA RCR-DRAW-002 ---
Name:         World-to-Pixel X Mapping
Formula:      wx(worldX) = centreX + worldX * scale
Output:       x_pixel  [px]  Canvas pixel X coordinate
Inputs:       centreX = W/2 + panX (canvas centre including pan offset)
              scale   = viewport scale (pixels per meter)

--- FORMULA RCR-DRAW-003 ---
Name:         World-to-Pixel Y Mapping
Formula:      wy(worldZ) = bedY - (worldZ - bedLvl) * scale
Output:       y_pixel  [px]  Canvas pixel Y coordinate
Inputs:       bedY = H * 0.70 + panY (reference Y for bed level)
              scale = viewport scale (pixels per meter)
Explanation:
  Canvas Y increases downward; real-world Z increases upward. Subtracting
  (worldZ - bedLvl) * scale from bedY converts elevation to screen pixels.

--- FORMULA RCR-DRAW-004 ---
Name:         World Metric to Pixel Size
Formula:      wm(meters) = meters * sf * 1000  [when sf = scale/40]
Alternative:  wm(px_ref) = px_ref * sf         [where px_ref is at 1:40 scale]
Output:       screen_px  [px]  Pixel equivalent of a real-world size
Usage:        Used for arrowhead lengths, text offsets, leader lengths, etc.

--- FORMULA RCR-DRAW-005 ---
Name:         Left Box Boundary X
Formula:      bxL = wx(-boxW / 2)
Output:       bxL [px]  Left boundary of the box culvert on canvas

--- FORMULA RCR-DRAW-006 ---
Name:         Right Box Boundary X
Formula:      bxR = wx(boxW / 2)
Output:       bxR [px]  Right boundary of the box culvert on canvas


SECTION 5: EMBANKMENT SLOPE AND BOULDER CUTTING FORMULAS
----------------------------------------------------------

--- FORMULA RCR-CUT-001 ---
Name:         Boulder Backing Width in Pixels
Formula:      bldPx = boulderW * scale       (boulderW = 0.6m)
Output:       bldPx [px]  Pixel width of the 600mm boulder backing column

--- FORMULA RCR-CUT-002 ---
Name:         Slope Vertical Extent in Pixels
Formula:      slopeWPx = yPccBot - bTop
Output:       slopeWPx [px]  Pixel height from top of box to PCC bottom
Note:         In canvas coordinates, yPccBot > bTop (larger Y = lower on screen)

--- FORMULA RCR-CUT-003 ---
Name:         Slope Height in World Meters
Formula:      slopeHM = slopeWPx / scale
Output:       slopeHM [m]  Real-world height of the 1:1 slope triangle

--- FORMULA RCR-CUT-004 ---
Name:         Boulder-to-Left-Bottom Intersection Y
Formula:      yHitBldLeft = bTop + slopeWPx - bldPx
Output:       yHitBldLeft [px]  Y pixel where the 1:1 slope hits the inner face of boulder backing

--- FORMULA RCR-CUT-005 ---
Name:         Embankment Slope X at Bottom Boundary (Left side)
Formula:      xHitBBot = bxL - slopeWPx + (bBot - bTop)
Output:       xHitBBot [px]  X pixel where the 1:1 slope reaches the bottom of the box

--- FORMULA RCR-CUT-006 ---
Name:         Slope Intersection with Cutting Plane
Formula:      xCut = (bxL - slopeWPx) + (bxL_ext - (bxL - slopeWPx)) * ((cutY - bTop) / (yPccTop - bTop))
Output:       xCut [px]  X pixel where the 1:1 embankment slope intersects the cutting plane
Inputs:
  bxL       = Left box face X (pixels)
  slopeWPx  = Slope horizontal extent in pixels (equals slope height for 1:1 slope)
  bxL_ext   = bxL - 0.15 * scale  (PCC extension left)
  cutY      = yWearTop = wy(bedLvl + wearC)  (cutting plane Y pixel)
  bTop      = wy(bedLvl + cH + tST)          (top of box Y pixel)
  yPccTop   = bBot = wy(bedLvl - tSB)        (bottom of box Y pixel, same as PCC top)
Explanation:
  Linear interpolation: The slope runs from (bxL - slopeWPx, bTop) to
  (bxL_ext, yPccTop). The cutting plane at cutY divides this line.
  The x coordinate of the intersection is computed by parameter t =
  (cutY - bTop) / (yPccTop - bTop) applied along the horizontal extent.
Mathematics:
  t = (cutY - bTop) / (yPccTop - bTop)
  xCut = startX + t * (endX - startX)
  where startX = bxL - slopeWPx, endX = bxL_ext

--- FORMULA RCR-CUT-007 ---
Name:         Boulder Polygon Morphing Condition
Formula:      IF xCut < (bxL - bldPx):
                Boulder polygon = 4-point rectangle
                Points: [(bxL-bldPx, bTop), (bxL, bTop), (bxL, cutY), (bxL-bldPx, cutY)]
              ELSE:
                Boulder polygon = 5-point clipped polygon
                Points: [(bxL-bldPx, bTop), (bxL, bTop), (bxL, cutY),
                         (xCut, cutY), (bxL-bldPx, yHitBldLeft)]
Explanation:
  When the embankment slope slices through the boulder backing column
  (600mm wide), the backing polygon must be clipped to the intersection.
  If the slope clears the column entirely, a simple rectangle suffices.
Source:       rcc-box-half-section-view.ts lines 963-987


SECTION 6: CELL BARREL LOOP FORMULAS
---------------------------------------

--- FORMULA RCR-CELL-001 ---
Name:         Cell X-Start Position (in world coords)
Formula:      cellX_start[0] = -boxW/2 + tOW
              cellX_start[i] = cellX_start[i-1] + cW + tIW   (for i = 1..cells-1)
Output:       cellX_start [m]  World x-coordinate of the left inner face of each barrel
Explanation:
  Starting from the left outer wall, offset by tOW to reach the first barrel
  inner face. Each successive cell starts cW + tIW further right.

--- FORMULA RCR-CELL-002 ---
Name:         Haunch Pixel Size
Formula:      hPx = haunch * scale
Output:       hPx [px]  Pixel size of the haunch (chamfer corner)

--- FORMULA RCR-CELL-003 ---
Name:         Cell Interior Clear Height Dimension Label
Formula:      dim_label = (cH - safe_wc) * 1000  [mm]
Inputs:       safe_wc = max(wearing_course, 0.150) [m]
Explanation:
  The dimension shown on the drawing for interior height is the structural
  clear height minus wearing course, expressed in millimetres.

--- FORMULA RCR-CELL-004 ---
Name:         Dynamic Haunch Leader Y Offset
Formula:      leaderBendY = bTop - (1.5 * scale)   [initial value]
              WHILE |leaderBendY - formY| < minClearance
                 OR |leaderBendY - railY| < minClearance:
                leaderBendY -= shiftStep
              leaderBendY = getSafeY(ctx, 'left', leaderBendY, shiftStep, minClearance)
Inputs:
  bTop          = wy(bedLvl + cH + tST)  (top of box in pixels)
  scale         = viewport scale
  minClearance  = 0.3 * scale  (0.3m real-world gap)
  shiftStep     = 0.1 * scale  (0.1m increment)
  formY         = wy(formLvl + wearC)
  railY         = wy(railLvl + wearC)
Explanation:
  The haunch leader is initially placed 1.5m above the top of slab. An
  iterative loop shifts it upward until it maintains at least 0.3m clearance
  from both the formation level and rail level callout lines.
Source:       rcc-box-half-section-view.ts lines 465-477


SECTION 7: PARAPET AND EARTH CUSHION DRAWING FORMULAS
-------------------------------------------------------

--- FORMULA RCR-EARTH-001 ---
Name:         225mm Parapet Gap Line Y
Formula:      line225Y = bTop - 0.225 * scale
Condition:    Only drawn IF earthCushionThickness > 0
Output:       line225Y [px]  Horizontal line above top slab indicating parapet gap
Explanation:
  Indian Railways requires a 225mm parapet gap between the top of the box
  culvert and the bottom of the ballast retaining wall / parapet above.
Source:       rcc-box-half-section-view.ts line 440

--- FORMULA RCR-EARTH-002 ---
Name:         Earth Cushion Hatch Region
Condition:    earthCushionThickness > 0
Top Y:        wy(formLvl)
Bottom Y:     bTop = wy(bedLvl + cH + tST)
Width:        Full box width bxL to bxR
Explanation:
  The fill between the top of the box and the formation level is hatched
  to represent compacted earth cushion.


SECTION 8: FOUNDATION LAYERS
------------------------------

--- FORMULA RCR-FND-001 ---
Name:         Granular Fill Layer Auto Height
Formula:      totalGranPx = yGranBot - yGranTop
              specifiedPx = sum of (layer.height/1000 * scale) for layers with explicit height
              autoCount = count of layers without explicit height
              autoPx = (totalGranPx - specifiedPx) / autoCount
Explanation:
  Foundation layers with user-specified heights occupy their exact pixel heights.
  Remaining auto-sized layers share the remaining pixel space equally.
Source:       rcc-box-half-section-view.ts lines 374-377
